Our story

Great businesses deserve to be seen.

Trinetra Creations started with a simple frustration. In market after market we saw the same thing: sweets made to family recipes, clinics with caring doctors, shops with honest prices, and signboards, menus and ads that said none of it.

So we set up a studio to fix that. We sit down with owners, listen to what makes them different, and turn it into a brand people remember: a logo, a voice, ads and packaging that all tell the same story.

Today we work with local shops, startups and growing businesses, from a single logo to a full launch campaign. The size of the job changes. The care doesn't.

Our name

Why Trinetra?

In Sanskrit, tri means three and netra means eye. In Indian tradition the third eye is the eye of inner sight: it sees what ordinary eyes miss. That's how we try to look at every brand.

  1. Vision

    We see where your brand could be, not just where it is today.

  2. Insight

    We look past the surface to what your customers really want and feel.

  3. Craft

    We turn what we see into design that is right down to the last detail.
