01

Logo & Identity

Your logo is the face of your business. We build a complete identity around it, so every signboard, visiting card and Instagram post looks like it came from the same confident brand.

What's included

  • Logo concepts, refined into one final mark
  • Colour palette and font pairing
  • Versions for every use: full, icon-only, one-colour
  • Visiting card, letterhead and signboard layouts
  • A simple brand guide (PDF) so anyone can use it correctly
  • All final files for print and web

Who it's for

  • New businesses getting ready to launch
  • Established shops whose logo looks dated
  • Businesses that look different on every sign, bag and page

How we work

  1. Discover

    We talk about your business, customers and competitors, and collect what you like and dislike.

  2. Design

    We explore several directions and show the strongest as concepts on real mock-ups.

  3. Refine

    You pick a direction; we fine-tune shape, colour and type with your feedback.

  4. Deliver

    You get every file plus the brand guide, ready for the printer and the web.

02

Ads & Campaigns

A good ad says one thing clearly and makes people act. We plan and design campaigns for festivals, launches and offers, in print and online.

What's included

  • Campaign idea and headline
  • Posters, flyers, banners and hoardings
  • Newspaper and magazine ads
  • Online ad banners in every size you need
  • Festival and seasonal offer designs
  • Print-ready files, sized for your printer or platform

Who it's for

  • Shops planning a festival sale: Diwali, Holi, Eid, New Year
  • Businesses opening a new branch or launching a product
  • Anyone whose ads aren't bringing in calls or visits

How we work

  1. Discover

    We agree the goal (footfall, calls or orders), the audience and the budget.

  2. Design

    One big idea and headline, shown as layouts in the real sizes.

  3. Refine

    We sharpen words and visuals until the message reads in three seconds.

  4. Deliver

    Final files for every size and medium, in time for your launch date.

03

Social Media

Your feed is often the first place customers meet you. We make it look consistent and sound like you, so people stop scrolling and start following.

What's included

  • Profile setup: picture, cover, bio and highlight covers
  • Post and story templates in your brand style
  • A monthly content calendar
  • Designed posts, carousels and reel covers
  • Captions and hashtag sets
  • Festival greetings planned in advance

Who it's for

  • Businesses on Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp with a mixed-up look
  • Owners too busy to post regularly
  • New brands building an audience from scratch

How we work

  1. Discover

    We review your pages and your competitors', and agree goals and tone of voice.

  2. Design

    Templates and a first month of posts, all in your brand style.

  3. Refine

    We adjust based on what you like and what your followers respond to.

  4. Deliver

    Ready-to-post designs and captions on a clear calendar, every month.

04

Packaging

On a crowded shelf you have a few seconds to be noticed. We design boxes, labels and bags that catch the eye and make the product feel worth its price.

What's included

  • Box, label, pouch and carry-bag design
  • Advice on shape, size and material
  • Product information layout: ingredients, MRP, FSSAI and barcode
  • Range design, so every flavour or size looks like one family
  • 3D mock-ups before anything is printed
  • Print-ready dielines, checked with your printer

Who it's for

  • Food, sweets and FMCG brands launching a product
  • Online sellers who want a memorable unboxing
  • Shops that want premium gift packs for festivals

How we work

  1. Discover

    We study your product, price point, shelf and competitors.

  2. Design

    Concepts shown as 3D mock-ups, side by side with competitors.

  3. Refine

    We polish the details and check every legal text and measurement.

  4. Deliver

    Print-ready files with dielines, checked with your printer before the first run.
