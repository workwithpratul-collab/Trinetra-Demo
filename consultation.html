Questions

Before you book

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. There's no fee and no obligation to hire us afterwards. We'd rather give you good advice today and earn your trust.

How long is the call?

Usually under half an hour. If we need to see your shop, packaging or pages, we'll ask you to share photos or links first.

I don't know what I need. Should I still book?

Absolutely, that's what the call is for. Tick "Not sure yet" in the form and we'll work it out together.

What happens after the call?

You get a short summary of what we suggest. If you'd like us to do the work, we'll send a clear quote. If not, no follow-up pressure.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to contact you about your request. We never sell or share them.
